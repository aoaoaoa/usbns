# Ideas bank

Treatments set aside for possible later use.

- **badi-ring/** The Badí‘ calendar drawn as a ring of 19 months in a year-long colour gradient, with each month's Feast and Holy Days in its popup. Briefly replaced by a flat year ribbon, then restored in the Two artists story (November 2026). The ribbon renderer (kind="ribbon", with Ayyám-i-Há as four gold dots) is now in `retired-renderers.py`. `badi-ring.png` is the rendered figure, `badi-ring.svg` its markup (styles not included), `ring_renderer.py` the generator branch. The renderer is still used for the full edition's "The year 183 B.E." chart.
- **life-in-years/** Louis Gregory's life as a grid of years: one filled square for each year he lived, outlined squares for each year since, events colored by kind (life, teaching journeys, service and institutions, writings, legacy) with popups. Shown briefly as No. 2 under the Gregory timeline (November 2026), alongside a two-column ledger of the same entries. `life-in-years.png` is the rendered figure; `renderer.py` holds both generator branches and their CSS. The data (GREGORY_EVENTS, GREGORY_KINDS) stays in data.py.
- **two-scales/** The Gregory timeline on a broken axis: his lifetime stretched across three-quarters of the width, the years after 1951 folded behind a break mark, year labels fanned out on leader lines so none collide, with a detail card below. From the timeline studies (November 2026). `two-scales.png` is the rendered figure, `two-scales.js` the drawing code, `two-scales-page.html` the full working studies page it came from.
- **retired-renderers.py** Generator branches removed from build.py in the November 2026 cleanup because no current data uses them: the life-in-years grid and ledger, the year ribbon, the pictogram, and the concept forms "path" and "strands". Each block pastes back into `chart_html` or `concept_html`.
- **badi-strip/** The Badí‘ dateline strip above the landing page’s header: today’s Badí‘ date and Gregorian date, the next Holy Day, and a Holy Days and Feasts panel (today’s month, the next four Holy Days and Feasts, tagged The Rhythm of Bahá’í Life). On the landing November 2026, removed for now; the editions keep the same calendar as a date chip in their top bars. `strip.html` is a working copy, `badi-strip.png` the open panel. To restore it, uncomment the `calendar_html()` line in `add_sections` in build/nsadd.py and rerun build/newsstand.py.
