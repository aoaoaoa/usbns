# The American Bahá'í — the arts issue, online

Three one-page sites built from the October/November/December 2026 issue of The American Bahá'í, a U.S. Bahá'í News Service publication. Each presents the issue's six features with its illustrations and photographs.

- `index.html` landing page.
- `folio/` the issue as one long scroll.
- `plates/` a dark gallery of the illustrations.
- `reader/` a reading app with a rail of features.
- `img/` art and photographs lifted from the issue PDF.
- `build/` the transcribed texts (`data.py`) and the generator (`build.py`; it expects the cropped images in `out/img/`).

Published with GitHub Pages from `main` via `.github/workflows/pages.yml`. Contents © 2026 National Spiritual Assembly of the Bahá'ís of the United States.
