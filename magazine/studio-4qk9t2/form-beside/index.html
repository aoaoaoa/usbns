Before you send

0 of 6 ready

  1. Send originalsFull-size files straight from the camera or phone, not screenshots or messaging copies.
  2. Name the photographerEvery picture carries a credit.
  3. Ask firstEveryone recognizable has agreed to be pictured. For children, a parent or guardian, and first names only.
  4. Say where and whenTown or neighborhood, state, and the date.
  5. VideoSteady and horizontal for the library; vertical welcome for social. Ten seconds per shot or longer.if you send any
  6. A few linesWhat happened and why it mattered, in your own words and in any language.

Five habits

  1. Be conscientious of your presence in the space
  2. Keep a steady hand
  3. Review your work on a bigger screen
  4. Capture a series of angles
  5. Be ready for spontaneous moments
