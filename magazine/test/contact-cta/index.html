Guidelines

Every item will be carefully considered, but we cannot guarantee publication.

Articles should be clear, concise and relevant to the aims of the current Plan. Limit: 800 words. We may edit stories for length and style. We cannot print advance articles to publicize locally sponsored events, but may be able to list them in the Calendar pages.

Photographs should be bright, well-composed and in focus. Please identify people when possible. Electronic images should be high resolution, in TIFF or JPEG format.

Have a story or photographs? Submit online with your BOSA login, or email TAB@usbnc.org.
