My light is in thee

Proposed

Where it goes

The Send a story popup, for a call in a later issue, in the same place as M1; or the receipt after sending.

What it does

A dawn rises from below and, one by one, lights a sky of stars that draw toward their neighbors: each person a lamp, each light joining the others.
