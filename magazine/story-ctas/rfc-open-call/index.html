You’re invited · Open call · Winter 2027

Education

The American Bahá’í is gathering stories, photographs and ideas for its Education issue. Whatever you have, there is a way to share it, and we’d be glad to hear from you.

We’re looking for

  • Children’s classes
  • Junior youth groups
  • Study circles
  • Tutor training
  • Seasonal Bahá’í schools
  • Learning wherever it happens

Five ways to take part

  1. A minuteShare an idea

    A sentence about something worth a closer look where you live.

    Feeds the editors’ list for this theme.

  2. A few minutesSend photographs or video

    Pictures or short clips from a class, a gathering, a project.

    May run as a microstory, on social media, or join the photo library for future stories.

  3. Half an hourPitch a story

    Who, where, what changed, and why it matters now.

    An editor writes back about where it could go.

  4. A few weeksWrite with us

    Report and write a story with an editor beside you.

    Published with your byline.

  5. OngoingJoin the contributor network

    Writers, photographers, videographers and local correspondents.

    Hear about themes first and work from the full guide.

  • No experience neededCare for your community is enough.
  • A phone is finePictures and clips straight from your pocket.
  • Any languageWrite or speak the way you think.
  • We work with youWe will get in touch and work with you before anything you share is used.

Where it can go

  • FeatureA full story in the magazine
  • MicrostoryA photograph or clip with a few lines
  • SocialShared on the magazine’s channels
  • Photo libraryB-roll and stills for future stories
