Neighborhood gatherings can be spontaneous, intimate and delicate. On a phone, wipe your lens and set your focus.

06Action

Be considerate of what you focus on. Match the shutter speed to the light and to the speed of the action.

07Devotion

Devotion takes different forms, and asks for creative, careful approaches.

08Candid and posed

Both are welcome; send some of each.

More examples. The Bahá’í Media Bank at media.bahai.org holds many good examples; it helps to look there first.

✶Before you send

1Send originalsFull-size files straight from the camera or phone, not screenshots or messaging copies.
2Name the photographerEvery picture carries a credit.
3Ask firstEveryone recognizable has agreed to be pictured. For children, a parent or guardian, and first names only.
4Say where and whenTown or neighborhood, state, and the date.
5VideoSteady and horizontal for the library; vertical welcome for social. Ten seconds per shot or longer.
6A few linesWhat happened and why it mattered, in your own words and in any language.
